# Vision & Mission

## Vision

The industry equates capability with scale: more parameters, more datacenter, more dollars per token. FlashMini starts from the opposite bet — that for the work developers actually do every day, discipline beats scale. Wrap a small model in verification, retrieval, and memory rigorous enough, and it stops mattering that the cloud assistant has a hundred times the parameters. One model, eight gigabytes, zero dollars per query, total privacy. That is the frontier FlashMini intends to kill.

## Mission

Build the most reliable small-model coding assistant in the world, and prove it in public:

1. **Private by default.** Code, memory, and logs never leave the machine.
2. **Verified by construction.** No claim of "fixed" without the verification command passing. Trust is earned by tests, not asserted by demos.
3. **Frontier-killer engineering.** Everything that makes giant-lab agents strong — strict tool calling, hybrid retrieval, persistent memory, bounded self-repair, full observability — engineered into an 8 GB budget. Scale is their advantage; discipline is ours.
4. **Open and reproducible.** MIT-licensed, documented, benchmarked. Every design decision has its reason written down.

**North star:** a published, repeatable head-to-head — FlashMini on a 4B model running locally, against frontier cloud assistants, on everyday tasks: bugfixes, multi-file edits, test-driven repair. Win the day-to-day, publish the numbers either way, and when a task genuinely needs frontier scale, say so and escalate instead of pretending. The model is replaceable; the discipline is the product.
